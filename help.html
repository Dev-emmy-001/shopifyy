Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our Shop, click on a product you love, select your size and quantity, then click "Add to Cart." When you're ready, go to your Cart and click "Proceed to Checkout" to complete your order with your delivery details and payment.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes! You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting us at support@dammygoldstore.com. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled, but you may return it after delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location. Lagos: 1–2 business days. Abuja & Port Harcourt: 2–3 business days. Other states: 3–7 business days. You can track your order on our Track My Order page.

How do I return an item?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached. Contact us at support@dammygoldstore.com with your order number and photos of the item to start the return process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Bank Transfer, USSD Mobile Banking, and Debit/Credit Cards (Visa and Mastercard). All transactions are secured and encrypted for your safety.

How do I use a coupon code?

On your Cart page, you will see a "Coupon Code" field. Enter your code and click "Apply." Valid codes are: SAVE10 (10% off) and DAMMYGOLD20 (20% off). Codes are case-sensitive and cannot be combined.

How do I reset my password?

On the Sign In page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email address. You will receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. DAMMYGOLD STORE uses industry-standard encryption to protect all your payment and personal data. We never store your card details on our servers. Your security is our top priority.
