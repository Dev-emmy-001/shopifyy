Free Delivery

Enjoy FREE delivery on all orders above ₦50,000 within Lagos, and above ₦80,000 for all other states in Nigeria. This offer applies automatically at checkout — no coupon code needed!

Order Processing

All orders placed before 12:00 PM (noon) on business days (Monday – Friday) are processed and dispatched the same day. Orders placed after noon or on weekends/public holidays will be dispatched the next business day.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an email confirmation with a tracking number to monitor your delivery.

Delivery Conditions

  • Deliveries are made during business hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday – Saturday.
  • Someone must be available to receive the package at the delivery address.
  • If delivery is missed, our courier will attempt redelivery within 24 hours. After two failed attempts, the order will be returned to our warehouse.
  • DAMMYGOLD STORE is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect addresses, natural disasters, or other factors beyond our control.

Packaging

All orders are carefully packaged to ensure your items arrive in perfect condition. Products are placed in protective packaging with a packing slip inside for your reference. If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 24 hours of receipt with photos of the damaged packaging and items.
